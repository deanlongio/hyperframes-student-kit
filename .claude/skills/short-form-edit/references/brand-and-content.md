Choose the editing mode independently:
- Speech-led: verified transcript and EDL; preserve meaning and full sentence
  endings; captions/graphics support the speaker. Use the configured voice provider.
- Nonverbal: visually inspect sources, anchor edits to actions and outcomes, one-line
  intro and intentional closing footage/overlay or end frame. No invented transcript,
  mandatory voiceover, dense captions, or compulsory CTA.
- Hybrid: transcript anchors for retained speech and visual anchors for silent
  passages, both on one timeline. Apply action framing and ending checks throughout.

For e-commerce, show the actual product, use/benefit, and accurate offer when supplied.
For B2B, show the problem, real workflow/demo or evidence, and relevant outcome.
Do not force either into a fixed formula or fabricate results to complete the story.
Use a CTA only when it serves the requested objective.

Reusable lessons from the accepted nonverbal drill:
- Ask about brand once; reuse the answer. Darker does not mean unreadable bodies.
- Select full exchanges before graphics. Keep limbs, target and recovery in frame.
- A short opener and deliberate ending can carry context without narration.
- Overlay panels are valid on silent footage. Use negative space that stays clear
  across the shot, not a position judged safe from only one frame.
- Plan the finish early: let action and copy settle; resolve/fade the score together
  with the picture. A final-frame hold can be intentional and must be recorded.
- Show credible skill through footage; avoid exaggerated danger, fake official
  drill names, speed tricks and decorative impacts.

Reference reuse: prefer `style-library/registry.json` for cards, `style-templates/`
for scene starting points. Legacy productions and private approved reels are optional
examples, not runtime dependencies. Borrow a useful mechanism, then apply the chosen
brand and inspect it at the actual aspect ratio. Do not copy other people's footage.

New project names should be lowercase kebab-case (for example `krav-maga-drill-02`).
